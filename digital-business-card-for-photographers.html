Why it matters in this line of work

Photography is bought with the eyes. The job of the card is to get someone from "who shot this?" to looking at your gallery in one tap, which a link does and a printed card does not.

It is also how you get booked at the moment of maximum enthusiasm. At a wedding or an event, guests who liked what they saw can scan a code, see the portfolio and land on your enquiry form before the evening is over.

Keep the card itself light and typographic. Let the portfolio carry the images — the card is the door, not the gallery.

What to put on it

  • A link to the portfolio, above everything. It is the only thing that decides the booking.
  • Instagram, prominently. Still where most photography clients browse and verify.
  • An enquiry or booking link. Catch interest while the shoot is still fresh.
  • Your studio mark or wordmark. Cleanly placed — restraint reads as taste here.
  • The kind of work you take. Weddings, portraits, product — saves mismatched enquiries.

Setting it up

  1. Open the builder and add your name, role and business.
  2. Add the contact routes clients actually use, plus your booking or enquiry link.
  3. Add your logo and brand colour so it looks like you, not a template.
  4. Share it by QR code, link or wallet pass — no app needed at the other end.

Frequently asked questions

What should a photographer's digital business card include?

A link to your portfolio, a prominent Instagram link, an enquiry or booking link, your studio mark and a line on the kind of work you take. Keep the card light and let the portfolio carry the images.

How do I share it at a wedding or event?

Show the QR code on your phone, a small print or a card at the table. Guests scan it with their camera app, land on your portfolio and can enquire — no app, and nothing for you to carry a box of.

Can I match it to my brand?

Yes. You can set your brand colour and add your logo or wordmark. Pro adds fuller custom branding and removes the CompanyCard credit if you want it completely white-labelled.
