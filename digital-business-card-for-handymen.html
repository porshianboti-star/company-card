How a handyman gets the next job, and what the card has to do

A handyman gets hired the moment something breaks. The homeowner is not researching; they are looking for the number of someone they trust, and the fastest way to be that someone is to already be saved in their phone. A paper card goes in a drawer. A card that was saved to their contacts in one tap comes up when they type the first three letters of your name.

The second way a handyman gets hired is a neighbour asking “who did your fence?” The answer used to be a half-remembered name. With a digital card it is a link forwarded in the same message, opening on your services, your photos and a tap-to-call button, with no app for the person receiving it.

And a handyman's details change more than most: a new number, a new service area after a move, drywall and tiling added to the list, a new insurance certificate. A printed card is wrong the week that happens. A digital card is edited once, and every QR code on the van, the invoices and the door hangers you have already handed out shows the current version.

What a handyman's card should carry

  • The jobs you take and the ones you do not. “Repairs, assembly, painting, drywall, fixtures and small carpentry — no roofing or gas” answers the first question a homeowner has, and saves the calls you would turn down.
  • Your service area. The towns or neighbourhoods you cover, so a forwarded card does not bring enquiries from an hour away.
  • Tap to call and tap to text. Most handyman jobs start with a photo of the problem sent by text. Make the number one tap and say which you prefer.
  • Photos of finished work. On the free plan the card carries your profile, links and socials, so link your Google Business Profile gallery, Facebook page or Instagram. Pro adds unlimited links and files, so a before-and-after sheet can sit on the card itself.
  • Where your reviews live. A link to your Google reviews, Nextdoor or whichever page your customers already use. CompanyCard does not host or verify reviews — link to the ones you have.
  • Insurance and licensing, as your own rules require. Handyman licensing differs by state, county and city, and some work is reserved for licensed electricians or plumbers. Show whatever your jurisdiction and your insurer expect, exactly as you show it on your quotes — CompanyCard does not check or supply it.

Setting up your card in ten minutes

  1. Open the builder and add your name or business name, your number, the jobs you take and the area you cover.
  2. Add links to your Google Business Profile, Facebook page or Instagram, and whatever insurance or licensing information your own rules require.
  3. Print the QR code on the van, your invoices and quotes, and any door hangers or flyers you already use.
  4. Paste the card link into your email signature and your text-message replies, and add the card to Apple or Google Wallet so it is one swipe away at the door.

Frequently asked questions

What should a handyman's digital business card include?

The jobs you take and the ones you do not, your service area, tap-to-call and tap-to-text numbers, photos of finished work, a link to where your reviews live, and whatever insurance or licensing information your own state, county, city and insurer expect you to show.

Do my customers need an app to open the card?

No. The card opens in any phone browser from a QR code or a link, and saving you to their contacts is one tap. Only you need an account.

What happens to the QR code on my van when I change my number or add a service?

Nothing needs repainting or reprinting. The QR code and the link point to your live card, so you edit the number or the service list once and every van sticker, invoice and door hanger you have already given out shows the new details.

Can I show photos of my work on the card?

Yes. On the free plan the card carries your profile, links and socials, so your Google Business Profile gallery, Facebook page or Instagram are one tap away. Pro adds unlimited links and files, so a before-and-after sheet can sit on the card itself.

Is it free for a self-employed handyman?

Yes. The free plan includes one digital business card, a QR code and sharing link, your profile, links and socials, an Apple and Google Wallet pass, and unlimited updates — free forever, no credit card. Free cards carry a small CompanyCard credit; removing it is part of Pro. Lead capture, analytics, custom branding and unlimited links and files are part of Pro.
